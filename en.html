Fit Journal — Privacy policy

2026-09-09

Fit Journal stores exercises, weights, workouts and wellbeing notes only on this device, in app storage. These records power your journal and statistics.

The app does not send these records to the developer or third parties. There are no accounts, ads, tracking or cloud sync. Your language choice is also stored locally.

Records remain until app data is deleted. The button below permanently deletes workouts, exercises, equipment and notes. Clearing app storage in Android settings also removes your language choice. Protect access to your device; the app does not separately encrypt its database.

If you email support, your email provider sends your address and message to the recipient. Do not send sensitive health information. The external policy website handles visits under its own privacy terms.

Fit Journal is a workout journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
